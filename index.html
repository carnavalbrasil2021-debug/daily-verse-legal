Daily Verse — Support

Need help with Daily Verse, a purchase, reminders or the widget? We are happy to help.

Contact

Email: darenight.app@outlook.com

Please describe the issue and include your device model and iOS or Android version. Never send passwords or payment information.

Common questions

Purchases. If Premium or Remove Ads does not appear, open Me > "Restore purchases". Payments are handled by Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). For refunds, visit Apple's Report a Problem site or Google Play refunds.

Daily reminder. Turn it on in Me > Daily reminder. If nothing arrives, check that notifications for Daily Verse are allowed in your phone's Settings app.

Widget (iPhone). Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget, search for "Daily Verse" and choose a size.

Your data. Prayers, notes and streaks are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
