Daily Verse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Daily Verse ("the app") is published by FG GLOBAL ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your prayers, gratitude notes, saved verses, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads may use a device identifier if you allow it.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm what you own.

Information stored on your device

Your prayer journal, gratitude entries, mood check-ins, saved verses, reading position, streaks, quiz scores, reminder time and other settings are saved locally on your device so the app remembers them. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Reminders and widget

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No push server is involved. The Home Screen widget shows the verse of the day using data stored on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, we show ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), IP address (used for approximate location), device type, and interactions with ads, to serve, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. If you do not allow tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalized using the advertising identifier. Users in regions that require it (such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states) are asked for consent and can change their choice at any time in Me > "Ad privacy choices". Your prayers and journal entries are never shared with advertisers.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

If you buy "Premium" or "Remove Ads", no ads are shown.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

All payments are handled by Apple through your Apple ID; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore them. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase history for this app and basic device information. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not read your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • We do not upload your prayers, notes or reading history to any server.

Children

Daily Verse is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not keep personal data on our servers, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. For data processed by Google or RevenueCat, you can use the controls described in their policies, reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings, or contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
